What happens next?

Once we receive your information, we'll contact you about membership dues, next steps, and ways to get involved.

  • Membership dues are based on employee count.
  • Members in good standing can be listed in the directory.
  • New members receive a complimentary quarter-page black-and-white ad in the Chamber directory, subject to current program details.

About the Chamber

Helping Haysville's business community thrive.

The Haysville Chamber of Commerce works to advance the commercial, industrial, and civic interests of Haysville and the surrounding area. We bring businesses together, support sound development, and help strengthen our community.
